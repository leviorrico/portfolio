Avelu

Avelu is a one-woman company that seeks to renovate women's confidence so they can achieve what they want. The brand aims to highlight the care necessary for the skin and believes that touching is the main resource to trasmit sensations. The naming and the logotype were created so to convey delicacy and softness, but at the same time be strong and incisive, due to their weight and high contrast in the shape of letters.
