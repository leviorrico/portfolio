Harmonics

To bring a bit of nostalgia, when we have the impression that things were more beautiful and happier before, the identity for the ceremonial band Harmonics was inspired by the 50's lettering style.


The logotype shows a happy curvature with irregular contrast in the trace of letters, mimicking the writing of a marker and brings even more movement. The swash in the letter H alludes to music notation. The illustrations, also inspired by the 50's, meld together musical instruments with objects usually found at weddings, such as dresses, suits, cakes and champagne.
