OHMA

Architecture and design studio, OHMA seeks above all the collaboration with its clients, suppliers and partners to express feelings in projects alive with color and sensitivity.


By bringing what is human through clean lines and aesthetics into what is merely utilitarian, OHMA's identity aims to let the projects speak for themselves.


Using as few strokes as possible, the logo is fragmented into parts that come together before our eyes to form a cohesive whole, alluding to the way the studio works.
